# Lumacast Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 8 November 2026_

Lumacast does not collect, store, sell or share any personal data.

- **No accounts, analytics, ads or tracking.** The app contains no third-party SDKs.
- **Local network only.** Lumacast advertises itself on your local Wi-Fi (mDNS/Bonjour) and receives the screen, video and audio that you choose to send from your own Apple devices. This stream is decoded and shown on your TV in real time and is never recorded, stored or sent anywhere else.
- **Device identifiers.** Lumacast generates a random receiver ID and an encryption key on first launch so that your devices can recognise it. Both stay on the TV and are deleted when you uninstall the app.
- **Permissions.** Network access (to receive streams), multicast (for discovery), "display over other apps" (so mirroring can open automatically), and start at boot (so the receiver is always available).

Lumacast is open source; you can verify all of the above at https://github.com/pappapaolo/lumacast.
